बिल्वाष्टकम् · Traditional

Bilvashtakam

Sung while offering bilva (bael) leaves. Every verse ends: "one bilva leaf, offered to Shiva."

Verse 1

त्रिदलं त्रिगुणाकारं त्रिनेत्रं च त्रियायुधम् । त्रिजन्मपापसंहारं एकबिल्वं शिवार्पणम् ॥

Tridalaṁ triguṇākāraṁ trinetraṁ ca triyāyudham, trijanma-pāpa-saṁhāraṁ eka-bilvaṁ śivārpaṇam.

Three leaves — the three qualities of nature; the three eyes; the three-pointed weapon. Destroyer of the wrongs of three lifetimes: one bilva leaf, offered to Shiva.

Verse 2

त्रिशाखैः बिल्वपत्रैश्च अच्छिद्रैः कोमलैः शुभैः । तवपूजां करिष्यामि एकबिल्वं शिवार्पणम् ॥

Triśākhaiḥ bilvapatraiśca acchidraiḥ komalaiḥ śubhaiḥ, tava-pūjāṁ kariṣyāmi eka-bilvaṁ śivārpaṇam.

With three-lobed bilva leaves — unbroken, tender, auspicious — I will perform your worship: one bilva leaf, offered to Shiva.

Verse 3

अखण्ड बिल्व पत्रेण पूजिते नन्दिकेश्वरे । शुद्ध्यन्ति सर्वपापेभ्यो एकबिल्वं शिवार्पणम् ॥

Akhaṇḍa bilva patreṇa pūjite nandikeśvare, śuddhyanti sarva-pāpebhyo eka-bilvaṁ śivārpaṇam.

When the Lord of Nandi is worshipped with a whole, unbroken bilva leaf, one is cleansed of every wrong: one bilva leaf, offered to Shiva.

Verse 4

शालिग्राम शिलामेकां विप्राणां जातु अर्पयेत् । सोमयज्ञ महापुण्यं एकबिल्वं शिवार्पणम् ॥

Śāligrāma śilām-ekāṁ viprāṇāṁ jātu arpayet, somayajña mahāpuṇyaṁ eka-bilvaṁ śivārpaṇam.

The merit of gifting a shaligrama stone, the great merit of the Soma sacrifice — all of it is in this: one bilva leaf, offered to Shiva.

Verse 5

दन्ति कोटि सहस्राणि वाजपेय शतानि च । कोटिकन्या महादानं एकबिल्वं शिवार्पणम् ॥

Danti koṭi sahasrāṇi vājapeya śatāni ca, koṭi-kanyā mahādānaṁ eka-bilvaṁ śivārpaṇam.

Ten billion elephants given away, a hundred Vajapeya sacrifices, the greatest of gifts — one bilva leaf, offered to Shiva, equals them.

Verse 6

लक्ष्म्याः स्तनत उत्पन्नं महादेवस्य च प्रियम् । बिल्ववृक्षं प्रयच्छामि एकबिल्वं शिवार्पणम् ॥

Lakṣmyāḥ stanata utpannaṁ mahādevasya ca priyam, bilva-vṛkṣaṁ prayacchāmi eka-bilvaṁ śivārpaṇam.

Born of Lakshmi herself and dear to Mahadeva — I offer the bilva tree: one bilva leaf, offered to Shiva.

Verse 7

दर्शनं बिल्ववृक्षस्य स्पर्शनं पापनाशनम् । अघोरपापसंहारं एकबिल्वं शिवार्पणम् ॥

Darśanaṁ bilva-vṛkṣasya sparśanaṁ pāpa-nāśanam, aghora-pāpa-saṁhāraṁ eka-bilvaṁ śivārpaṇam.

To see the bilva tree, to touch it, ends wrongdoing; it destroys even the most terrible faults: one bilva leaf, offered to Shiva.

Verse 8

काशीक्षेत्र निवासं च कालभैरव दर्शनम् । प्रयागे माधवं दृष्ट्वा एकबिल्वं शिवार्पणम् ॥

Kāśī-kṣetra nivāsaṁ ca kālabhairava darśanam, prayāge mādhavaṁ dṛṣṭvā eka-bilvaṁ śivārpaṇam.

To live in Kashi, to behold Kala Bhairava, to see Madhava at Prayaga — all of that fruit is here: one bilva leaf, offered to Shiva.
