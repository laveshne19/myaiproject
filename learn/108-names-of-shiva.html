Ashtottara Shatanamavali

108 Names of Lord Shiva

Recited as “Om … namaḥ”, one name to a bead of the mala. These are the hundred and eight ways the tradition has found to say the same thing.

  1. शिव Shiva — The auspicious one — goodness itself, not merely a being who is good
  2. महेश्वर Maheshwara — The great lord, ruler of all
  3. शम्भु Shambhu — The source of joy; the one who is happiness itself
  4. पिनाकी Pinaki — Bearer of the Pinaka bow
  5. शशिशेखर Shashishekhara — Who wears the moon as His crest
  6. वामदेव Vamadeva — Beautiful and pleasing in every aspect
  7. विरूपाक्ष Virupaksha — Of the odd-numbered eyes — the three-eyed
  8. कपर्दी Kapardi — With thickly matted hair
  9. नीललोहित Nilalohita — Blue and red — the colours of His form
  10. शंकर Shankara — Giver of peace and prosperity
  11. शूलपाणि Shulapani — Who holds the trident in His hand
  12. खट्वांगी Khatvangi — Who carries the skull-topped staff
  13. विष्णुवल्लभ Vishnuvallabha — Dearly beloved of Vishnu
  14. शिपिविष्ट Shipivishta — Whose form is light itself
  15. अम्बिकानाथ Ambikanatha — Consort of Ambika, the Mother
  16. श्रीकण्ठ Shrikantha — Of the glorious throat, stained blue by poison
  17. भक्तवत्सल Bhaktavatsala — Tender toward His devotees as a cow to its calf
  18. भव Bhava — Existence itself
  19. शर्व Sharva — Who removes all suffering
  20. त्रिलोकेश Trilokesha — Lord of the three worlds
  21. शितिकण्ठ Shitikantha — Of the dark blue throat
  22. शिवाप्रिय Shivapriya — Beloved of Shiva (Parvati) — and dear to the pure of heart
  23. उग्र Ugra — Fierce, terrible when needed
  24. कपाली Kapali — Who bears the skull, having taken on the debt of Brahma’s head
  25. कामारि Kamari — Enemy of desire — who burned Kamadeva
  26. अन्धकासुर सूदन Andhakasura Sudana — Slayer of the demon Andhaka
  27. गंगाधर Gangadhara — Bearer of the Ganga in His locks
  28. ललाटाक्ष Lalatataksha — With an eye set in the forehead
  29. कालकाल Kalakala — The death of death itself
  30. कृपानिधि Kripanidhi — A treasury of compassion
  31. भीम Bhima — Awe-inspiring, of formidable presence
  32. परशुहस्त Parashuhasta — Who holds the axe
  33. मृगपाणि Mrigapani — Who holds a deer in His hand — the restless mind, held gently
  34. जटाधर Jatadhara — Who wears matted locks
  35. कैलासवासी Kailasavasi — Dweller on Mount Kailash
  36. कवची Kavachi — Armoured
  37. कठोर Kathora — Unyielding; who ripens all things to their end
  38. त्रिपुरान्तक Tripurantaka — Destroyer of the three cities
  39. वृषांक Vrishanka — Whose emblem is the bull
  40. वृषभारूढ Vrishabharudha — Who rides upon the bull
  41. भस्मोद्धूलित विग्रह Bhasmoddhulita Vigraha — Whose body is smeared with sacred ash
  42. सामप्रिय Samapriya — Who loves the Sama Veda and its chanting
  43. स्वरमय Svaramaya — Made of sound; the source of the musical notes
  44. त्रयीमूर्ति Trayimurti — Embodiment of the three Vedas
  45. अनीश्वर Anishwara — Who has no lord above Him
  46. सर्वज्ञ Sarvajna — All-knowing
  47. परमात्मा Paramatma — The supreme Self
  48. सोमसूर्याग्नि लोचन Somasuryagni Lochana — Whose three eyes are the moon, the sun and fire
  49. हविः Havi — The oblation offered in every sacrifice
  50. यज्ञमय Yajnamaya — Made of sacrifice; the yajna itself
  51. सोम Soma — Who is with Uma; also the nectar of the moon
  52. पञ्चवक्त्र Panchavaktra — Of the five faces
  53. सदाशिव Sadashiva — The eternally auspicious
  54. विश्वेश्वर Vishveshwara — Lord of the universe — the Kashi form
  55. वीरभद्र Virabhadra — The heroic one born of His wrath
  56. गणनाथ Gananatha — Lord of the ganas, His attendant hosts
  57. प्रजापति Prajapati — Progenitor and protector of all creatures
  58. हिरण्यरेता Hiranyareta — Of golden seed — the source of creation
  59. दुर्धर्ष Durdharsha — Who cannot be overcome or approached by force
  60. गिरीश Girisha — Lord of the mountains
  61. गिरित्र Girisha (Giritra) — Protector of the mountains and of those who dwell there
  62. अनघ Anagha — Without fault, untouched by wrongdoing
  63. भुजंगभूषण Bhujangabhushana — Ornamented with serpents
  64. भर्ग Bharga — Radiance that burns away impurity
  65. गिरिधन्वा Giridhanva — Whose bow is the mountain Meru
  66. गिरिप्रिय Giripriya — Who loves the mountains
  67. कृत्तिवासा Krittivasa — Clothed in the elephant hide
  68. पुरारि Puraratha — Enemy of the demon cities
  69. भगवान् Bhagavan — The one possessing all glory
  70. प्रमथाधिप Pramathadhipa — Chief of the pramathas, His fierce attendants
  71. मृत्युंजय Mrityunjaya — Conqueror of death
  72. सूक्ष्मतनु Sukshmatanu — Of subtle body, finer than the finest
  73. जगद्व्यापी Jagadvyapi — Who pervades the whole world
  74. जगद्गुरु Jagadguru — Teacher of the world
  75. व्योमकेश Vyomakesha — Whose hair is the sky
  76. महासेनजनक Mahasenajanaka — Father of Kartikeya, commander of the divine army
  77. चारुविक्रम Charuvikrama — Of graceful valour
  78. रुद्र Rudra — The roarer; who drives away sorrow
  79. भूतपति Bhutapati — Lord of all beings and of the elements
  80. स्थाणु Sthanu — The immovable pillar; firm and unchanging
  81. अहिर्बुध्न्य Ahirbudhnya — The serpent of the depths — the foundation
  82. दिगम्बर Digambara — Clothed in the directions — sky-clad
  83. अष्टमूर्ति Ashtamurti — Of eight forms: the five elements, sun, moon and the sacrificer
  84. अनेकात्मा Anekatma — Of many selves — appearing as all
  85. सात्त्विक Sattvika — Of pure and luminous quality
  86. शुद्धविग्रह Shuddhavigraha — Of a wholly pure form
  87. शाश्वत Shashvata — Eternal, without beginning or end
  88. खण्डपरशु Khandaparashu — Who wields the broken axe
  89. अज Aja — Unborn
  90. पाशविमोचक Pashavimochaka — Who unties the noose that binds beings
  91. मृड Mrida — Who shows only mercy
  92. पशुपति Pashupati — Lord of all bound creatures
  93. देव Deva — The shining one
  94. महादेव Mahadeva — The great god
  95. अव्यय Avyaya — Imperishable, never diminishing
  96. हरि Hari — Identical with Vishnu; the remover
  97. भगनेत्रभिद् Bhaganetrabhid — Who put out the eye of Bhaga at Daksha’s sacrifice
  98. अव्यक्त Avyakta — Unmanifest, beyond perception
  99. दक्षाध्वरहर Dakshadhvarahara — Destroyer of Daksha’s sacrifice
  100. हर Hara — Who takes away — sin, sorrow, and finally the self
  101. पूषदन्तभिद् Pushadantabhid — Who broke the teeth of Pushan at the yajna
  102. अव्यग्र Avyagra — Never agitated, steady in all conditions
  103. सहस्राक्ष Sahasraksha — Thousand-eyed
  104. सहस्रपाद Sahasrapad — Thousand-footed — present everywhere
  105. अपवर्गप्रद Apavargaprada — Bestower of liberation
  106. अनन्त Ananta — Endless
  107. तारक Taraka — The ferryman who carries beings across
  108. परमेश्वर Parameshwara — The supreme lord
