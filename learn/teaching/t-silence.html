The Highest Teaching Was Given in Silence

Dakshinamurthy sits under a banyan facing south, young, and the four sages before Him are ancient. He teaches them without saying a word, and they understand.

This is a deliberate statement about the limits of explanation. Everything that can be transmitted in words has already been written down, and the writing has not made many people wise.

In practice this is less mystical than it sounds. Fifteen minutes of genuine silence a day — no phone, no music, no company, no task — reorganises how your mind runs within a few weeks. Most people have not had fifteen unbroken silent minutes in years, and mistake that condition for their personality.

Practice

Fifteen minutes. Sit. No input. The discomfort in the first week is the point, not a sign that it is not working.
