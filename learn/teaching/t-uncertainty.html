Living Without Knowing

The trident holds three points at once — past, present, future — in one hand, and Shiva Himself stands outside all three as Mahakala.

You can hold the lessons of the past, the demands of the present and the possibilities of the future without being run by any of them. Most anxiety is one of the three colonising the other two: the past pretending to be the future, or the future pretending to be now.

Uncertainty is not the enemy of good action. It is the only condition in which courage means anything.

Practice

Write down the decision you are avoiding. Write the worst realistic outcome. Write what you would do next if it happened. Most avoidance dies at step three.
