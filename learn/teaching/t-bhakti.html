You Do Not Have to Be Good at This

The last verse of the Rudrashtakam — the one that makes people cry in temples — is Tulsidas admitting he knows nothing:

"I do not know yoga. I do not know japa. I do not know how to perform worship. I only know how to bow."

Every tradition eventually accumulates experts, and then people who are suffering start to feel unqualified to approach. Shaivism keeps this verse in the middle of its most-recited hymn as a permanent correction.

The boy Upamanyu had no technique. Markandeya just held on. The bilva leaf is worth more than a hundred sacrifices.

Practice

Do the smallest sincere thing today. One bilva leaf. Do not upgrade it into a project.
