The Race Around the World

Shiva set His sons a contest: circle the world three times. Kartikeya mounted his peacock and flew. Ganesha walked around his parents and sat down, and said — you are my world.

He won. And his brother, who had done the harder thing correctly, felt cheated and left for the south, and did not come back.

Both sons are right, and the story does not resolve it. Cleverness wins the prize; effort is left holding the effort. If you are the brother in this story, the tradition does not tell you to be gracious about it. It tells you where he went, and that he became beloved there.

Practice

If you have been out-manoeuvred by someone cleverer, stop relitigating. Ask instead: where are the hills that are mine?
