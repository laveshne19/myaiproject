The Power of No

Shiva owns nothing worth listing. Ash, a skin, a drum, a begging bowl. He is the wealthiest figure in the pantheon by the only measure that counts: there is nothing that can be taken from Him.

This was not poverty. It was subtraction as a strategy. Every commitment you hold consumes attention whether or not you are attending to it.

Practice

Find one commitment that is consuming you and serving nothing. End it this week, cleanly, without a story.
