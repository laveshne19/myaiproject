What Repair Actually Costs

Shiva beheaded His own son at the door of His own house because He did not recognise him.

He does not then explain that He had good reason, or that the boy started it, or that He was tired. He gives Ganesha permanent precedence over Himself in every ritual in India, forever.

That is the ratio. Repair is not proportional to your intentions; it is proportional to the damage. Most apologies fail because they are priced at the offender’s guilt rather than the other person’s loss.

Practice

Think of one repair you have made cheaply. Ask what the honest price would have been. Consider paying the difference.
