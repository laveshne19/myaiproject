The Third Eye Is Just Paying Attention

The third eye is not a superpower you unlock. It is viveka — discrimination — the faculty that separates what is actually happening from the story you are running about what is happening.

Notice that in the Puranas Shiva opens it exactly once, and it is the most destructive act He performs. The capacity to see through something completely is also the capacity to burn it. That is why it stays closed.

You already have this. It engages every time you pause before reacting, every time you ask whether you are seeing clearly or seeing what you fear.

Practice

Before your next hard decision, write the situation twice: once as you feel it, once as a stranger would describe it in a police report. The gap between them is the work.
