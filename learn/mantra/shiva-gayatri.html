The Gayatri addressed to Mahadeva

Shiva Gayatri

ॐ तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे महादेवाय धीमहि । तन्नो रुद्रः प्रचोदयात् ॥

Oṁ tatpuruṣāya vidmahe mahādevāya dhīmahi, tanno rudraḥ pracodayāt.

What it means

We know that Supreme Person; we meditate on the Great God. May Rudra illumine our understanding. It is a prayer not for things, but for clear seeing.

Chanted for

Clarity in decisions, discernment, study and concentration.

How to chant it

The traditional count is 108 repetitions — one full mala. Sit facing east or north, light a lamp if you can, and let the sound settle rather than hurrying it. Shiv AI counts the beads for you and rings the bell every twenty-seven.
