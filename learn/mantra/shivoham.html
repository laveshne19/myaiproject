Shivoham

शिवोऽहम् शिवोऽहम्

Śivo’ham, Śivo’ham

What it means

I am Shiva. Not "I worship Shiva" — I am That. This is the boldest line in the tradition: the auspicious consciousness you are looking for is not elsewhere, and never was.

Chanted for

Self-worth, dissolving the sense of being small, non-dual meditation.

How to chant it

The traditional count is 108 repetitions — one full mala. Sit facing east or north, light a lamp if you can, and let the sound settle rather than hurrying it. Shiv AI counts the beads for you and rings the bell every twenty-seven.
