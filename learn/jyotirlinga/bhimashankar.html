Bhimashankar Jyotirlinga

Where: Sahyadri hills, Pune district, Maharashtra

Where Shiva destroyed the demon Bhima, son of Kumbhakarna, and the sweat of that battle became the Bhima river. The temple is Nagara style in black stone, set inside a wildlife sanctuary that is the last stronghold of the Indian giant squirrel. The walk up through the forest is part of the darshan.

When to go

Monsoon and just after, when the Sahyadris are green; Maha Shivaratri.
