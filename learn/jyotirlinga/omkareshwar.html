Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga

Where: Mandhata island, Khandwa district, Madhya Pradesh

An island in the Narmada shaped like the syllable Om. Two lingas are worshipped here — Omkareshwar on the island and Mamleshwar across the water — and pilgrims complete a parikrama of the whole island. Adi Shankaracharya is said to have met his guru Govindapada in a cave on this bank as a boy.

When to go

Kartik Purnima; the Narmada parikrama season, winter.
