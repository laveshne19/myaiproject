Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga

Where: Nashik district, Maharashtra

At the source of the Godavari, below Brahmagiri hill. Its linga is unusual — three small faces in a hollow, for Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva, worn low by centuries of water. This is the principal site for the Narayan Nagbali and Kaal Sarp Dosh rites, and pilgrims come here specifically for release from ancestral obligation.

When to go

Shravan Mondays; Simhastha Kumbh; Maha Shivaratri.
