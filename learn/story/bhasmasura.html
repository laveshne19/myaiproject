Bhasmasura, and the Boon That Ate Him

भस्मासुर

The demon Vrikasura performed severe austerities and Shiva, who has never been good at refusing a genuine devotee, granted him a boon. The demon asked that whoever he placed his hand upon should turn instantly to ash.

Shiva granted it. Bhasmasura immediately reached for Shiva.

Shiva ran. The god who drank the world’s poison ran across the earth from a monster of His own making, and it is one of the few genuinely embarrassing passages in the Puranas, and the tradition keeps it in.

Vishnu took the form of Mohini, the most beautiful woman in existence. Bhasmasura stopped chasing and started courting. Mohini said she would only marry a man who could match her in dance. He agreed. She danced, and he mirrored her exactly, move for move, and at a certain point she placed her palm on her own head, and so did he.

What it asks of you

Two things. First: generosity without discernment arms the wrong people, and even God gets caught by this. Second, and more useful: the demon was not destroyed by a superior force. He was destroyed by his own power, applied to himself, at the moment he stopped paying attention because he was flattered. Vanity is what makes someone imitate without thinking. If you cannot outfight the thing that is coming for you, let it copy you.
