The Sage Who Tried to Walk Between Them

भृंगी

The sage Bhringi was devoted to Shiva absolutely, and only to Shiva. When he came to Kailash to circumambulate the Lord, he refused to include Parvati in the circuit. He took the form of a bee to fly between them.

Parvati, angered, withdrew from him the part of his body that came from the feminine principle — flesh and blood — and Bhringi collapsed, a bag of bones. Shiva, in pity, gave him a third leg to stand on.

Standing there on three legs, he still would not honour her.

So Shiva ended it: He took her into Himself and became Ardhanarishvara, one body, half woman. There was no longer a path between them to walk.

What it asks of you

You do not get to accept the half of something you find congenial. Not in a marriage, not in a parent, not in your own nature — the disciplined half and the tender half, the ambition and the rest. Bhringi’s devotion was genuine and it was still a mutilation. And Shiva’s answer is worth sitting with: He did not argue with the sage. He simply removed the possibility of the separation.
