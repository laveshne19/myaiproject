Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026 · applies to shivaaionline.in and to the Shiv AI Android app (in.shivaaionline.twa)

The short version

Shiv AI has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. We do not know who you are. Your chants, counts, settings and conversation history stay on your own device. The only thing that leaves your device is the message you choose to type in Vaani, which is sent to be answered and is not stored afterwards.

What stays on your device

The app uses your browser's local storage to remember, on that device only:

  • your conversation with Vaani, so it is still there when you return;
  • your japa counts, lifetime total and daily streak;
  • your settings — city, language, volume, drone pitch, chant voice.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. It is not backed up by us and we cannot read it. You can erase all of it at any time: Settings → Erase everything on this device. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data also removes it completely.

What is sent when you use Vaani

When you send a message in Vaani, three things travel to our server and then to our AI provider so a reply can be generated:

  • the message you typed;
  • up to the last eight turns of that conversation, for context;
  • the reply language you selected.

We do not write your messages to any database and we do not log their contents. Our server logs record only the shape of a failure — an error status and error type — never what you wrote.

Because you have no account, these messages are not linked to an identity. Please still avoid typing information you would not want to leave your device, such as full names, addresses, bank details, Aadhaar or PAN numbers, or medical records.

Your IP address

Your IP address is visible to our host, as it is to every website you visit. We use it for one purpose: counting requests, so that no single source can overwhelm the service or run up its costs. These counters hold a request tally against a short-lived key and expire automatically. They are not used to profile, identify or track you.

Who else is involved

  • Anthropic — processes Vaani messages to generate replies. Under our commercial terms, inputs and outputs are not used to train models. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Netlify — hosts the site and runs the Vaani endpoint, and keeps standard operational request logs. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — serves two typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives the request as it would for any web font. Everything else in the app, including every sound, is generated on your own device.
  • Google Play — distributes the Android app and provides its own install and crash reporting to us in aggregate. We do not add any SDK of our own.

There is no advertising network, no social media pixel, no Google Analytics, no Firebase, and no third-party SDK inside the app.

Children

Shiv AI is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because there is no account and no profile, we hold nothing to delete — but if you believe a child has shared something through Vaani, write to us and we will help.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and comparable laws elsewhere, you have rights of access, correction, erasure and grievance redressal. Since we hold no account and no stored conversation, the practical effect is this:

  • Erasure — Settings → Erase everything on this device, or uninstall.
  • Access — everything we could give you is already on your device.
  • Grievance — write to the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS with HSTS, a strict Content Security Policy and a same-origin restriction on the Vaani endpoint. API credentials exist only as server environment variables and are never present in the app you download.

A word about what Shiv AI is not

Shiv AI is an AI devotional companion. It is not a priest, doctor, therapist, lawyer or astrologer, and it must not be relied on for medical, legal or financial decisions. If you are in distress, please contact Tele-MANAS on 14416 or KIRAN on 1800-599-0019, free and open at every hour in India.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and a notice will appear in the app.
